## Exact invariance lemma for permitted pure assignments

For a legal reassignment of already completed local cores, assume the following multisets and stock are identical to the source construction:

- the same three source-identified completed-core calls per port;
- the same source-frontier, target-frontier, and residual-gauge multisets;
- the same two width-two data complements per port;
- the same three-way orthogonal grouping of every residual `sigma` of width `a` into one width-`3a` exterior child; and
- the same full persistent role stock `W0` and ambient parameter `m`.

Match each source-identified child in the first assignment with that same child in the second. Each match preserves its width, including the complement children and the `3a` grouped exterior children. Thus the complete child multisets agree, `n'_r=n_r` for every width `r`, and `W0'=W0`. The source ledger is

```text
W0 = 2v + R
n_r = 3(H_r + D_r + T_r) + 2v * [r=2] + sum_(a>0) s_a * [r=3a]
```

where `H_r`, `D_r`, and `T_r` count the local internal, source-frontier, and target-frontier children, and `s_a` counts residual gauges of width `a`. The normalized moment therefore obeys

```text
M'(alpha) = (1/W0') sum_r n'_r (r/m)^(1-alpha)
          = (1/W0)  sum_r n_r  (r/m)^(1-alpha)
          = M(alpha).
```

The lemma is an exact statement about source-defined child multisets, not a claim about all possible covers. A finite router constant may differ while this normalized moment remains equal; that can affect finite guards or row thresholds, but by itself is not a recursive supplier saving.
