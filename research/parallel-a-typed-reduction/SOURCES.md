Section 03 uses source and target banks indexed by triples, separate bit and Gaussian-dyadic scalar domains, and a schedule that restores arbitrary dirty scratch. Section 04 charges a recursive full-array interchange by `s` calls on streams of volume `V/W`, requiring the all-role endpoint identity. Section 06 already has normalized synthetic Fourier transforms over `C[y]/(y^r+1)`, a retained frequency layout, approximate grid arithmetic, and signed Kronecker packing implemented with four exact integer products. The finite-field NTT below is a separate exact arithmetic path; it does not claim a new transform principle or reuse the source's Section 03/04 child recurrence.

The manuscript repository keeps its Apache-2.0 license. No manuscript code, upstream certificate, or prior checker is copied here. The fork's existing `LICENSE` and `NOTICE` remain unchanged.
