## Structural comparison checklist only

The pinned [balanced construction](https://redirect.github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/163) is not an interchangeable profile. Its own ledger reports a fused complex word, compensated physical handoffs, 15,681 virtual roles despite 13,041 physical roles, its own 24/72 bit word and operation-frame changes, the full rare-class fallback, atom toll and paid balanced positional transfer. Its positional router pays both layout directions; all data, spectators, scratch, phase, control and restored-row fields travel. Its own full group, scalar, row and 47-constraint bills remain attached to those separate suppliers. This pilot uses those points as a review checklist only; it imports no stock, frames, dimension, word, moment, router price or `kappa` from that construction.
