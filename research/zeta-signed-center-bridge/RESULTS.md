# Results

## Primary status: SIGNED_CENTER_IS_EXISTING_STAR_METHOD

The factorization is exact when X=∑_{S∈P_4}x_S is supplied. The checker verifies all 16 h=4 triple coefficients and all 1,024 P_4, h=8 coefficients, plus deterministic dyadic vectors. This is the PR144 coordinate-star B scatter written using Z_i=X−S_i; it proves no multiplication improvement.

The pinned PR144 graph also has an unselected center_kind="disjoint" branch with complementary centers and the factor ∑_i E_i/(h−3)−(1/2)∑_{i∈T}E_i. That is exactly the proposed formula after eliminating X. At h=8 it divides by 5, which is not in ℤ[i,1/2]. Supplying a direct total avoids that quotient but has no paid P_4 carrier. Literal support ranks are 56 for the complement centers plus 8 for X, versus 48 for the selected stars; these are not physical charges without frame proofs.
