## The two ledgers that must not be conflated

The original Section 3/4 bit supplier is a fixed full-array contract. Its production parameters are \(h=100\), \(v=161700\), \(N=v^3\), \(m=1000000\), and \(W_{\mathrm b}=177176569091445000000\). Its rational edge-rank sum is \(s_{\mathrm b}=177176569088785861287000000\), and the recurrence charges \(s_{\mathrm b}\) recursive child calls plus the linear movement and tape work at each node. Every scratch role, spectator field, source/sink correction, and row restoration is in this interface.

The pinned WHT Bridge2 geometry instead has \(h=22\), \(v=1320\), \(m=110\), \(R=9412\), and \(W=4v+R=14692\) live roles per group. Its own exact ledger saves \(D=4v-5c_{\mathrm{st}}=5280-2200=3080\) moves per unit, with \(c_{\mathrm{st}}=440\) copy moves per invocation. The WHT proof reports \(1,613,040\) moves instead of \(1,616,120\) per unit and \(\delta=7.474547\times10^{-4}\). It still pays the five invocations, helper copies, idle climbs, frame blocks, and other RAM costs. These values are not a new \(W,m,s\) for Section 4 and are not a multiplication \(\kappa\).

## Required matched ledger

Any continuation needs both the original and proposed all-role ledgers in the same source type and recurrence:

- complex and bit data children by width/rank, plus source, target, copy, center, residual, exterior, compensation, and endpoint corrections;
- role stock \(W\), ambient \(m\), rank mass \(s\), deficit \(D=mW-s\), maximum child, and paid moment \(M(a)=(mW)^{-1}\sum_r n_r r(m/r)^a\), where the original assembly is proved applicable;
- every interstage frame, pointwise gate compatibility, scratch owner and consumer, bank allocation, whole-bank packing, data transport, and decoder cost;
- fixed-tape selector, routing, prime, precision, recovery, and all-size costs or explicit remaining hypotheses.

The required mechanism-level signal is a proven change to a paid data, copied-center, or residual/dirty child family. If the child families and centers remain fixed, only the restricted barriers above apply, and stage names do not change the recurrence.
