## What this establishes

The local carrier has a real invertible GF(2) boundary map. Section 4's
recursive call is generated by a rational address-frame edge rank after
factorization; its child permutes address chunks in one complete row-class
array, with parent and child copying, ownership, descriptor, dirty-state,
and cleanup charges. The local scalar map does not provide that edge data.

The PR 63 compiler internally forms source-node blocks, but the serialized
physical word omits their node/use identities. The saved transition and
profile files are aggregate records. A saved rank mass or XOR total therefore
cannot be charged to this edge. The manuscript's `m=1,000,000` bit-network
constants and the PR 63 profile's `m=575` are different source objects; their
rank sums and child rows are not interchangeable.

This finding is limited to the selected source use and frozen artifacts. It
does not prove a general impossibility, change the scalar recurrence, or
support a new `κ` claim.
