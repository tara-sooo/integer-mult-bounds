## What is proved

- The exact integer formula satisfies \(P_{AB}^2=I\), maps every source
  label in the fixed fibre to its \(\tau\) partner, has determinant
  \((-1)^{h/2}\), and has rank \(h/2\) over \(\mathbb Q\) and
  \(\mathbb F_2\). The inverse-transpose transports the dual
  annihilators. Exact finite checks cover every third triple at \(h=4,6\).
- For the ten independent \(h=6\) contexts, the direct sum has rank 30
  over both fields. Every invertible relabeling of that same action
  keeps rank 30.
- A lane-indexed \(E,D\) is lossless for all 20 independent X and Y
  arrays and arbitrary dirty scratch. It only reindexes storage; it
  does not share a frame or reduce the same-operation rank.
- The exact stage-3 bit gate witness in GATE_AND_DECODER.md shows
  \(P_{AB}\) maps the terminal source line correctly but fails to map
  the full original gate label. The complex \(2^m\)-entry array frame
  and Section 4 all-role bit endpoint also remain unconstructed.
