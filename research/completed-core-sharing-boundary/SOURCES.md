## Academic sources

- Xiande Zhang and Gennian Ge, “Maximal resolvable packings and minimal resolvable coverings of triples by quadruples,” *Journal of Combinatorial Designs* 18 (2010), 209–223, Lemmas 2.2 and 2.4. DOI: [10.1002/jcd.20234](https://doi.org/10.1002/jcd.20234). PR #128's signed grouping generator implements and checks this construction; the paper is not redistributed.
- D. Yu. Grigor'ev, “Additive Complexity in Directed Computations,” *Theoretical Computer Science* 19 (1982), 39–67, Appendix A.2, Proposition 14(c). DOI: [10.1016/0304-3975(82)90014-7](https://doi.org/10.1016/0304-3975(82)90014-7). The original Section 4 cites this for lower-triangular factorization.
