# Results

## Primary status

**`ADAPTER_OVERHEAD_ERASES_GAIN`** — scoped to the proposed unshared per-class adapter route with the original $(W,m)$ and no proved deletion of an old multiplication child.

The rank-one map is an exact unimodular involution over integers and rationals and an invertible involution in characteristic two. It swaps the two source lines and its inverse transpose maps the dual target hyperplanes correctly. The pinned [PR 209](https://redirect.github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/209) final gate also admits an exact local frame-aware Gate C identity when the **actual** Section 3 array-frame transitions are used.

The local gate does not establish the requested full multiplication bridge. The matrix $P$ on the $m$-dimensional label space is not itself the Section 3 array-frame operator or the Section 4 edge between the existing bit frames. The complex lift costs two residual-vector factors each way in the finite fixture; the original bit source and sink frame differences have rational rank two. The all-role bit endpoint, full ρ routing, complete data decoder, and original dirty-scratch map remain missing.

Even granting only two rank-one transitions for every class, the full-cover diagnostic adds $N$ rank mass and changes the old deficit from $2{,}659{,}138{,}713{,}000{,}000$ to −1,568,813,400,000,000. The existing bit-frame hop screen adds $2N$ for one role; including both the source and dual paths gives a known unchanged-frame screen of $4N$ and $D-4N=-14{,}252{,}669{,}739{,}000{,}000$, before extra roles or decoding. Neither is a full recurrence. [PR 209](https://redirect.github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/209)'s WHT invocation saving does not identify an eliminated multiplication child to offset either cost. No κ improvement is claimed.
